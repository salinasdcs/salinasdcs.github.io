Exciting news: my paper ["Credit Limits and Consumption Behavior over the Life Cycle"](/workingpapers/) has been **accepted for publication** in the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-review-of-economics-and-finance">International Review of Economics &amp; Finance</a>. The paper studies how stochastic borrowing limits erode households' capacity to self-insure against idiosyncratic risk over the life cycle.
